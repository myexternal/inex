(02) How it's made

It starts as flat steel.

Scroll to build it

  1. 01 / 04

    Raw steel

    Stainless sheet, square tube, cast burner rings and brass gas valves. That is all a wok range is before we start.

  2. 02 / 04

    Cut, fold, weld

    Each sheet is sheared and folded on the press brake, then welded into one rigid body. This is our two-burner wok range.

  3. 03 / 04

    Fit the line

    On site, the range takes its place between tables, burners and sinks, under a hood made to the length of your wall.

  4. 04 / 04

    First service

    Gas on, flames up. We test every burner and stay until it all works.

Illustration of our process. The wok range is modelled on a real Inex unit.

(04) How a kitchen gets made

Five steps. One crew.

0102030405
  1. Step 01

    Measure

    We come to your site, measure the room and talk through how your kitchen cooks.

  2. Step 02

    Draw

    Every unit is drawn to your room, your gas line and your menu before any steel is cut.

  3. Step 03

    Cut

    Stainless sheet is marked and sheared to size on our floor.

  4. Step 04

    Fold & weld

    The press brake folds each panel, and the crew welds it into a unit.

  5. Step 05

    Finish & fit

    Every unit is finished and checked on our floor. Then we fit it, connect it, test it and stay until it works.
